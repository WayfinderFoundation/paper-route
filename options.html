Wayfinder API keys will only work once agents and shells have been fully restored. Until then, connect with a private key. After that, they need a strategy or policy wallet: session wallets expire after 15 minutes, and an API key can't refresh them.

Your key isn't stored. Paper Route uses it once to set up a trading key and approve the builder fee, then forgets it. Nothing that can withdraw is saved.

The trading key is encrypted (AES-GCM) with a device key that can't be exported from this browser profile, and kept in the extension's own storage. It can trade on your Hyperliquid account but can't withdraw or transfer. There's no Paper Route server: everything runs in your browser.

Open papertrade

Go to papertrade and sign in. Paper Route uses whichever papertrade account you're signed in to, and its panel appears on the trade page. Turn on Tether there, then place trades on papertrade's own order ticket as usual: Paper Route hedges each one on Hyperliquid as it fills.

Stop loss and take profit are set in papertrade's order form or on any open position. Tether hedges on Hyperliquid and shines with large positions riding big moves; hedged, a papertrade loss earns Paper while Hyperliquid wins it back.
