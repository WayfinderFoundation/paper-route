Paper Route hedges with a trading key it set up on this wallet. It can place orders but can't withdraw or transfer. You'll only paste your key again to send USDC to papertrade, or to reconnect after 180 days.

Paste the private key of the Hyperliquid wallet you'll hedge with. Paper Route uses it once to set up a trading key that can trade but can't withdraw, then forgets it.

Wayfinder API keys need a strategy or policy wallet (session wallets expire after 15 minutes, and an API key can't refresh them).

Open papertrade

Go to papertrade and sign in. Paper Route uses whichever papertrade account you're signed in to, and its panel appears on the trade page. Turn on Tether there, or open a hedged trade.
